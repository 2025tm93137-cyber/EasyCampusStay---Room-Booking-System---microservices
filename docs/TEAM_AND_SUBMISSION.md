# Complete your real team and submission details

Run scripts/fill_submission_details.py to enter the team name, actual students, IDs, responsibilities, contribution summaries, GitHub link, Google Drive folder and video link. The values are saved in docs/submission.json and used by the PDF builder.

Every member must make genuine contributions and speak in the final video. A baseline imported under one account does not prove that every member authored it. Keep contribution descriptions and commit references accurate. Faculty must be able to open the submitted repository and video.

The report remains a draft while names or required links are missing. After entering final details and generating current evidence, rebuild with tools/build_documents.py, review the PDFs, commit the real changes and push.
